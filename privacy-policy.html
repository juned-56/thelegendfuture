Privacy Policy

Introduction

At The New Future Legend, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, partners, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our construction and consulting services.

By using our website and services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy, in accordance with the Royal Decree No. 6/2022 (Personal Data Protection Law) of the Sultanate of Oman.

Information Collection

We collect information to provide better services to our clients. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical address provided during consultations or via our contact forms.
  • Project Data: Technical specifications, architectural requirements, and site metadata necessary for project planning.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you navigate our digital platforms, including IP addresses, browser types, and interaction timestamps.

Data Utilization

The information we collect is used for the following strategic purposes:

  • Executing project management and construction logistics.
  • Communicating critical project updates and administrative notices.
  • Enhancing the performance and security of our digital infrastructure.
  • Ensuring compliance with Omani construction and safety regulations.
  • Developing high-precision BIM models and digital twins for asset management.

Security Measures

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your data. This includes:

  • End-to-end encryption for all digital project communications.
  • Secured on-site data servers for architectural and structural blueprints.
  • Strict internal access controls limited to essential project personnel.
  • Regular vulnerability assessments of our smart building management systems.

Compliance & Governance

Legend Construction adheres to the highest standards of international data governance. We do not sell, rent, or lease our client lists to third parties. Disclosure of information to government authorities will only occur when legally mandated or necessary to protect the safety of our sites and communities.
