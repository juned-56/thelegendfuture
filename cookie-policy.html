Cookie Policy

Our Digital Stewardship

The New Future Legend utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site performance, and provide secured access to our professional portals (such as the BIM and Project Management hubs).

This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your rights to manage them in accordance with Omani and international digital standards.

What are Cookies?

Cookies are high-density text files containing small amounts of data which are downloaded to your device when you visit a website. They allow the platform to "remember" your preferences and structural configurations between visits.

Categorization of Usage

We deploy cookies in the following strategic categories:

Category Function
Essential Necessary for the website to function, including secure portal logins and CSRF protection.
Performance Analytical tracking (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with our portfolio and service pages.
Functional Remembers your language preferences and localized metadata for a personalized experience.

Managing Your Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default, but you can typically modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer.

  • Browser Settings: You can manage cookie settings via your browser's "Options" or "Preferences" menu.
  • Impact of Refusal: If you choose to reject essential cookies, some parts of our high-precision digital portals may become inaccessible.

Third-Party Technologies

We may integrate third-party services (such as architectural visualization tools or interactive maps) that set their own cookies to monitor performance and user interaction. These are governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

Policy Evolution

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Modified" date.
