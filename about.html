Who We Are

Building Dreams
For 14 Years

The New Future Legend is a story traversing over 14 Years and today is one of the fastest-growing building business groups in Muscat, Oman.

With a focus on the builder of residential, Commercial and Government Projects to offer premium and luxury housing, we have constructed various projects in premium locations within the established areas of Muscat and Barka.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to provide uncompromised quality and engineering excellence to every client. Today, we stand as a symbol of trust and luxury in the Omani construction landscape.
